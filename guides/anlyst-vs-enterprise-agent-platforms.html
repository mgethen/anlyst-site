Anlyst vs enterprise agent platforms

Anlyst Global Intelligence. Updated November 2026.

The short answer

Enterprise AI agent platforms for finance are built for banks and large funds: priced for a bank, deployed by engineers, and built around Office. Anlyst is built for the boutique and the mid-market firm: priced for a boutique, set up by a partner in a week, and built around the tools a boutique already runs on. An enterprise platform sells finished outputs. Anlyst holds the deal.

Two products for two kinds of firm

The large platforms in this category serve institutions with hundreds of users, house formats to enforce and engineering teams to work with the vendor. They are sold through an enterprise process, and the vendor's engineers build and maintain agents for each deliverable. For a bank, that is the right shape.

Most deals are not done by banks. They are done by boutiques and mid-market advisors: a handful of partners, a small team, a live mandate and no IT department. That firm needs a different product, not a smaller licence.

Upstream and downstream

Reviewing a finished deck for numbers that do not tie is valuable. It is also late. Anlyst does its checking at the front: The Gate verifies what comes in, The Register holds every figure with its document and page, and every later work product inherits the same verified facts. The two approaches are not rivals. Verifying what goes in and reviewing what comes out are two halves of the same chain.

Which one is right for you

  • An enterprise platform suits a firm with hundreds of users, strict house formats and people on staff to work with the vendor's engineers.
  • Anlyst suits a firm where the partners run the deals, the team is small, and the first week of every mandate is still spent moving numbers around.

Questions people ask

Is Anlyst a smaller version of an enterprise agent platform?

No. The unit is different. An enterprise platform is organised around a user and the documents they produce. Anlyst is organised around the engagement, kept as a persistent object with its facts, questions and versions.

Do I need engineers or an IT team to use Anlyst?

No. A partner sets it up in a week, with the firm's own documents ingested and the Kickoff report in hand.

How is Anlyst priced?

Anlyst is priced for a boutique rather than a bank. We are working with a small number of design partner firms; talk to us for terms.

Can a firm use both?

Yes. Verifying what goes into a deal and reviewing the finished documents are complementary.
