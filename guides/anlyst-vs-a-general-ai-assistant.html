Anlyst vs a general AI assistant

Anlyst Global Intelligence. Updated November 2026.

The short answer

A general AI assistant is very good at the task in front of it: a brief, a draft, a summary. It does not hold the deal. Anlyst is the intelligence and memory layer underneath. It reads every deal document once, keeps the verified facts with their sources, and gives your assistant those facts to work from. You keep using the assistant you already use. Now it knows the deal.

What a general assistant does well

Deal teams already use general assistants every day, and with good reason. They prepare people for meetings, build first target lists and turn notes into drafts. Individual tasks get faster. That is useful, and it is incremental: the work is quicker, but the deal is still held in people's heads, in folders and in spreadsheets.

Where it stops

A general AI assistant is a brilliant intern on a three month rotation. Every deal, you start by teaching it the firm again.

  • It does not hold the deal. Each conversation starts from whatever you give it. The facts, the open questions and the last version of the numbers are not kept anywhere the next person can rely on.
  • It does not show its working. A figure in a draft is the model's reading of a document. It does not arrive with the page it came from.
  • Nothing stops it assuming. A missing unit, a scale, a legal name: a general assistant will often fill the gap and carry on.
  • The NDA may rule it out. Where an NDA bars processing in a public AI service, the client's documents cannot go there at all.

What Anlyst adds

  • The Gate. Nothing gets into a deal unverified, and nothing is assumed. A legal name, a currency, a scale comes from a source that states it, or is raised as a question.
  • The Register. Every figure, cited to its document and page. The model reads and the code calculates: ratios and cross foots are never left to the model.
  • Kickoff. What the room holds, and what it means, in a day: the report and the first question list.
  • Memory. The deal remembers, so the firm does. Nothing the firm learns is lost when a deal closes or a person leaves.

You do not have to choose

Anlyst is not another chat window. It works inside the tools and the assistant you already use. Ask your assistant for the brief before a meeting and it answers from the deal's verified facts. Save the note afterwards and the deal keeps it.

Questions people ask

Can I get the same result with a well set up project in my AI assistant?

A well set up project will carry one person through one deal. It will not give the firm a register of cited facts, a question list that tracks its answers, or a record that survives the person who built it.

Is Anlyst a replacement for my AI assistant?

No. Anlyst sits underneath it. You keep using the assistant you already use, and it works from the deal's verified facts and their sources.

What happens to client documents?

Each firm's data is walled off from every other firm's. Documents reach a model only when the engagement is authorised for it, every model call is logged, and nothing is used to train a shared model. The assistant sees facts with their sources, never the documents.

Who is Anlyst built for?

M&A advisory boutiques and mid-market advisors first, then investment funds and corporate development teams.
