The boundary

Not a general assistant with a deal prompt. The engagement is a persistent object: facts with provenance, questions with answers, versions with deltas. The assistant sees the facts you ask for, never the documents.

Trust by design

Every fact carries its source, its period and its status.

01

Source

Document and page, attached to every figure, always one click from the original.

02

Basis

Audited, management, restated or forecast. The basis travels with the number and is never mixed silently.

03

Computation

Ratios, growth rates and cross foots are calculated in code, never by the model, and checked against the source statements.

04

Anomalies

Flagged and written up as the question list for the first management call, never smoothed over.

05

Nothing assumed

A legal name, a currency, a jurisdiction, a scale: each is taken from a source that states it, or left absent and raised as a question. Never defaulted, never inferred from a filename or a letterhead. The legal name comes only from a company registry extract.

06

Isolation

Each firm's data is walled off from every other firm's. Documents go to a model only when the deal is authorised for it, every call is logged, and nothing trains a shared model. Deal and firm memory belong to the firm.
