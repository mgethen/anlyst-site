Trust by design

Every fact carries its source, its period and its status.

01

The model reads. The code calculates.

Every figure is one click from its page. Ratios and cross foots are computed in code, never by the model. Nothing is assumed: a legal name, a currency, a scale comes from a source that states it or is raised as a question.

02

Your data stays yours

Each firm's data is walled off from every other firm's. Documents reach a model only when the engagement is authorised for it, every call is logged, and nothing trains a shared model.

03

Not a chat window

Anlyst works inside the tools and the assistant you already use. The assistant sees the facts you ask for, with their sources, never the documents.
